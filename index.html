About Our School


Jai Prakash Narayan Sarvodaya Vidyalaya is a government senior secondary school located in Gopalpur, Bhojipura, Bareilly, Uttar Pradesh. Affiliated with the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE), the school is committed to providing quality education to students from the local community.

The school's philosophy is rooted in the ideals of Sarvodaya — the welfare of all. We strive to build an inclusive, supportive learning environment where every student can flourish regardless of their background.

Admissions

We warmly welcome students seeking quality education in a disciplined, nurturing school environment.

1

Eligibility

Students must meet the age and academic eligibility criteria as per CBSE and UP Government norms for the respective class.

2

Application

Visit the school office to collect or enquire about the admission form. All forms must be filled and submitted as directed.

3

Document Submission

Submit all required documents along with the completed admission form to the school office within the specified period.

4

Confirmation

Admission will be confirmed subject to seat availability, eligibility verification, and completion of the admission process.

View Admission Information

📄 Required Documents

  • Birth Certificate
  • Transfer Certificate (TC) from previous school
  • Mark Sheet / Report Card of previous class
  • Aadhaar Card (Student)
  • Passport-size photographs
  • Caste Certificate (if applicable)
  • Residence proof / Address proof

ℹ️ Important Note

Admission dates, fees and seat availability are subject to the school's current schedule and UP Government guidelines. Please visit or contact the school office for the most current information.
